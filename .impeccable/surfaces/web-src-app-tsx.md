# Surface brief: Lumina app + landing

Scope: the desktop/web app (Operate) at web/src, and the landing site (Persuade) at site/. One world for both.

Audience: professionals and students in online/in-person meetings and classes. App job: take shorthand while listening, get enhanced notes, review, chat over sources. Landing job: copy the install command.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The split view is the Cornell method made live: the user's shorthand sits in the cue column, Lumina's enhanced notes fill the body row-aligned to each cue, the summary is written at the foot. Refuses the category default of a grey sidebar plus a document with a transcript drawer, and the dark neon "AI app".

OWN-WORLD: White page on a cool blue-grey desk ground; a committed Cornell-blue rail (#2B4FA8 family) as the notebook cover; ink #1E2430; one blue rule colour for structure, selection and links; margin red (#D9453B) reserved for the cue/body boundary and the live recording state; highlighter yellow only for detected questions (Live Assistance); green only for indexed/ready. UI in Atkinson Hyperlegible Next; enhanced notes in a text serif (Source Serif 4); tabular figures for times. No shadows-as-cards, no borders where a colour field or rule does the job.

STORY: The visitor sees their own words first, Lumina's expansion beside each one, and the red margin line that tells them Lumina is listening. They trust it because every enhanced line traces back to a cue and a transcript moment, and nothing leaves the machine.

FIRST VIEWPORT: App: blue rail left (New meeting, Meetings, Sources, Chat, Settings); page centre with title + time row on top, cue column ~32% left of the red margin rule, enhanced body right, each enhanced block aligned to its cue row; summary band at the foot under a double blue rule; live transcript strip below the page with You/Others speaker labels; Live Assistance cards stack in a right gutter in highlighter yellow. Landing: the page itself is one Cornell sheet: cue column holds nav and keywords, body holds the pitch with a running demo (shorthand typed in the cue column expands in the body while the margin rule pulses red), the summary band at the foot of the first viewport holds the install command with a copy button and OS tabs.

FORM: Cornell Page, position 1 on my ordered list (pick card, chosen by the user over the rolled Spectrum Program); seed key 47deb3b1. Signature move: the red margin rule is alive while recording (elapsed-time fill runs down it), and enhanced blocks align row-for-row to cues.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
