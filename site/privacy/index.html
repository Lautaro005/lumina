Privacy policy

What stays on your computer, and the few times Lumina uses the network.

Effective October 22, 2026. This policy covers the Lumina app (the local server, the web interface, the desktop app and the lumina command) and this website.

In short

  • Lumina has no servers, no accounts and no telemetry. The author receives nothing about you or your use.
  • Your recordings, transcripts, notes, summaries, embeddings, chats and indexed files stay on your computer.
  • Lumina connects to the internet only to download models and updates, to search the web when you turn web search on, and to reach a model server you configure.

What stays on your computer

Lumina processes audio and text with models that run on your device (Parakeet, Canary, Whisper, Silero VAD, EmbeddingGemma 2, Gemma 4 through llama.cpp, Kokoro). It stores everything in its data folder, ~/.lumina/data by default: the SQLite database with meetings, transcripts, notes and chats, the ChromaDB search index, downloaded models, uploaded files, settings and logs. Audio is transcribed as it arrives and is not saved as an audio file, except recordings you import yourself, which are copied into uploads/.

The Lumina server listens only on 127.0.0.1 and refuses requests from other machines and from websites open in your browser. The MCP server is available to agents you set up on the same computer; the HTTP endpoint requires a token.

Ollama, LM Studio and the built-in Gemma 4 run on your computer, so nothing leaves it when you use them. Third-party services have their own privacy policies.

Recording other people

Lumina can record the other participants of a call through your computer's audio. Laws on recording conversations differ by country and state; some require everyone's consent. Tell the people you meet with when you record them. Lumina never starts recording on its own: it records only after you click Start listening and stops when you pause or end the meeting.

Your control

  • Delete a meeting, a source or a chat from the app; it is removed from the database and the search index.
  • Delete downloaded models from Settings.
  • Remove everything by deleting the data folder. lumina uninstall removes the command and the app and keeps your data until you delete it.

This website

The website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts. The only thing stored in your browser is your light/dark preference if you change it. GitHub, as the host, may process technical data such as your IP address to serve the pages, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Lumina does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are listed in the changelog and in the repository history. Questions: open an issue on GitHub; security problems go to the security policy.
